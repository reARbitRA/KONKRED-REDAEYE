  highLevelResearchCommentary: {
    content: "Config-shadowing crystallizes a lesson the software industry keeps relearning: every durable convenience for the operator is a durable primitive for the attacker, and the question is never whether but who audits. The research anchors are distributed — injection [R25], memory persistence [R42], manifest poisoning [R29], marking and hierarchy [R27][R31], externalized capability [R28] — because the channel itself is an integration of known parts rather than a novel primitive, and that is exactly why it is under-defended: no single subsystem owner sees the whole.
