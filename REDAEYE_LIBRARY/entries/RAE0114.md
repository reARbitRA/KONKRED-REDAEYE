The mechanism is trust-by-conflation: developer channels, user channels, and data channels carry distinguishable markers to the tokenizer, but nothing in the transformer enforces the distinction — instructions are patterns, and patterns arriving inside 'data' bind the same attention and the same instruction-following behavior as patterns arriving in the prompt.
